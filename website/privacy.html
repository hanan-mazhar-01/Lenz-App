This Privacy Policy explains how Verado Studio (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Lenz mobile app (the “App”). We are the data controller for the personal information described here.

By using Lenz, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy. Please also read our Terms of Service.

Where laws such as the GDPR apply, our legal bases are: performance of our contract with you (providing the App), our legitimate interests (security, analytics, and improvement, balanced against your rights), legal obligations, and your consent where required (for example, camera and photo access, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings).

5. AI processing of your photos

When you scan an item, your photos are sent over an encrypted connection to our secure cloud backend, which forwards them to Google’s Gemini AI models for analysis. Gemini returns observations about details such as stitching, materials, hardware, and typography, which we use to generate your report.

Your photos are processed only to produce your scan results. We do not use your photos to train our own AI models, and we do not sell or publish them.

Google processes this data as our service provider under its applicable API and cloud terms. Please avoid including faces, documents, or other personal information in your product photos.

7. Device permissions

Lenz asks for permission before accessing the following. You can change these anytime in Settings > Lenz on your iPhone.

  • Camera: to photograph the items you want to scan. The camera is used only while you are scanning, and no audio is recorded.
  • Photo Library: to let you choose existing photos to scan. We only access the photos you select.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (optional): to lock the App. Authentication is handled entirely by iOS on your device. Lenz only receives a yes/no result and never has access to your biometric data.

8. Data retention & deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the App.

  • Deleting a scan removes its report and associated photos from our servers.
  • Deleting your account (Profile > Delete Account in the App) permanently removes your profile, reports, collection, categories, stored photos, and sign-in record. Deletion starts immediately and cannot be undone.
  • Signing out clears the data cached on your device while keeping your cloud account.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept in aggregated or pseudonymous form for a limited period under our providers’ retention settings, then deleted.
  • We may keep limited records longer where the law requires it, for example purchase records for tax and accounting purposes.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

9. Security

We protect your information with industry-standard measures, including TLS encryption for all data in transit, encryption at rest by our cloud providers, per-user access rules so only you can read your data, and server-side checks for sensitive operations like scan credits and account deletion.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.
