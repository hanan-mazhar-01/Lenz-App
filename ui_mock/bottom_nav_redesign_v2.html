Bottom navigation — 2 new concepts

Both are built around what Lenz is: a detective with a magnifier. Same five destinations and behaviour as today (Home · History · Scan · Saved · Profile). Each is shown on a standard 390 px phone and on a small 320 px phone. Tap the tabs and the scan button.

Concept 1 — Lens dock. A glass magnifier ring slides under the active tab and enlarges its icon, so the “current place” is literally Lenz looking at it. The scan button is a camera-aperture shutter in a gold ring; the aperture twists when pressed. Bright, ivory, calm — it sits on the app’s light screens like a piece of the app.

Concept 2 — Mascot rider. A docked deep-forest bar where Lenz himself (the same animated character as the rest of the app) peeks over the active tab and hops to the next one when you switch. The gold scan lens pulses softly. It gives the nav personality no other app has, and it reuses the existing mascot, so there is nothing new to draw.

Both keep the 5 slots, labels, 44 px+ touch targets, safe-area padding, the hide-on-full-screen-layer rule and reduced-motion support (the lens / mascot then jump instead of gliding). Tell me which one and I will build it in Flutter as a drop-in for LenzTabBar.
