Bottom navigation — 3 variations

Same five destinations (Home · History · Scan · Saved · Profile), same behaviour. Tap any tab or the scan button to feel the motion. Each one is drawn over a Home screen so you can judge it in context.

Notes for the build. All three keep the 5-slot layout, 44 px+ touch targets, and respect the home-indicator safe area (the bar sits 18–20 px above it, or pads it for B). Hiding rule is unchanged: the bar slides away while a full-screen layer (scan, report, paywall) is open. Motion: 450–500 ms ease-out for the pill / indicator, spring (1.56) for the lift, and everything collapses to an instant change when “reduce motion” is on. Pick one and I will build it in Flutter as a drop-in replacement for LenzTabBar (same constructor).
