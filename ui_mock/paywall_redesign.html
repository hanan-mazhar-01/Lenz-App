Paywall — one screen, no scrolling

Shown on four phone sizes at once, from a small 320×568 to a large 430×932. The mascot is the only flexible element, so it simply gets smaller on small phones — every plan, the button and the links stay visible. Tap a plan to see the button and fine print change; tap “Billing details” for the legal text.

What changed. The old paywall stacked hero → benefits → three plans → button → legal block, which needed a scroll on most phones. Now: benefits are three compact icon tiles, Yearly is one wide card (with the free-trial copy that already comes from the store), Monthly and Weekly sit side by side, and the legal paragraph moved behind Billing details. Prices, trial text and plan names stay dynamic (from the store offering), so nothing is hard-coded — the mock only fills them in so you can see the layout. Required items stay on the page: price + billing period next to the button, Restore, Terms and Privacy links.
